How successful was the French Revolution?

Requirements: use at least 4 words from the word box · write 4–6 sentences.

1 · The plan remember: Claim → Success → Failure → Big picture

1. ClaimAnswer the question: it was partly successful.
2. SuccessEnded Divine Right; Rights of Man; Napoleonic Code (equality before the law).
3. FailureReign of Terror; Napoleon became emperor; Congress of Vienna brought kings back.
4. Big pictureIts ideas inspired revolutions everywhere (Haiti, Latin America).

    4 · Facts to have ready

    • Third Estate — 97% of France; paid almost all the taxes
    • 1789 — Tennis Court Oath; Bastille; Declaration of the Rights of Man
    • Rights of Man — "Men are born and remain free and equal in rights"
    • 1793 — Louis XVI executed by guillotine
    • Reign of Terror (1793–94) — Robespierre; about 17,000 officially executed
    • 1799 / 1804 — Napoleon seizes power / crowns himself emperor
    • Napoleonic Code — equality before the law (but fewer rights for women)
    • Congress of Vienna (1814–15) — restored kings and the balance of power
    • Legacy — inspired Toussaint L'Ouverture (Haiti) and Simón Bolívar
    • Other sides you could argue: "mostly successful" (ideas lasted) or "mostly a failure" (ended with an emperor and a king again). Any side is OK if you back it up.
